# One Login

A mobile-first web app for sole-trader trade contractors showing monthly financial position and a single primary workflow to record completed jobs.

## Language

**Job**:
A completed piece of work performed for a customer with an earned monetary amount.
_Avoid_: Task, ticket, project, order

**Customer**:
The individual or organization for whom a job was completed.
_Avoid_: Client, buyer, account

**Financial Position**:
The current month's aggregate figures: Money In, Money Out, and Profit.
_Avoid_: P&L, balance sheet, financial statement

**Money In**:
Total gross revenue recorded as income in the current calendar month.
_Avoid_: Revenue, sales, turnover, incoming

**Money Out**:
Total expenses recorded against the business in the current calendar month.
_Avoid_: Cost, overhead, outgoings, expenditure

**Profit**:
Money In minus Money Out for the current calendar month.
_Avoid_: Margin, net income, bottom line

**In the Black**:
Visual state when Profit is greater than or equal to zero, presented with clear emerald/green styling and indicators.
_Avoid_: Positive balance, surplus

**In the Red**:
Visual state when Profit is negative, presented with urgent crimson/red styling and indicators.
_Avoid_: Deficit, loss
